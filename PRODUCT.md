# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is anyone exploring criticism of the current higher-education system, especially students who recognize its failures. Visitors need to move through a large, non-linear body of arguments, examples, proposed alternatives, and external sources without first learning Obsidian.

## Product Purpose

The site publishes the project's JSON Canvas as a fast, accessible, public exploration surface. Success means a visitor can immediately understand where to begin, pan and zoom confidently, read individual ideas comfortably, and follow supporting sources even when a third-party site cannot be embedded.

## Positioning

The argument remains spatial and connected instead of being flattened into a conventional article or trapped inside an Obsidian export.

## Brand Personality

Direct, provocative, and intellectually serious. The interface should feel confident and candid while getting out of the way of the ideas.

## Anti-references

Do not reproduce the heavy, slow, visually noisy Obsidian HTML export. Do not imitate the earlier `unis-bad` experiment or turn the canvas into a generic marketing landing page.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the author's spatial composition and the open JSON Canvas source.
- Make the starting point obvious without forcing a linear reading order.
- Keep navigation fast enough that the interface disappears into exploration.
- Treat external sources as durable references, not fragile iframe-only content.
- Use direct language and restrained visual emphasis to support a serious argument.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a strong web baseline: keyboard-operable navigation, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, touch-friendly controls, screen-reader labels, and a usable non-canvas reading fallback.
